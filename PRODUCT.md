# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Guests who know when they need a rental and want to reserve a specific vehicle from one rental business.

## Product Purpose

Let a guest enter pickup and return dates, choose an available vehicle, review the pickup details and price, and receive a booking reference after the reservation is saved. Payment is due at pickup.

## Operating Context

The business uses one pickup and return location. Rental dates follow that location’s time zone and include both endpoints. The review shows the total due and any configured fee or deposit before collecting the guest’s name, email, and phone.

## Product Principles

- Show the individual vehicle’s public label so guests can distinguish vehicles of the same make and model.
- Keep the guest’s dates when availability changes so they can choose again.
- Show the booking reference, vehicle, dates, location, and amount due on confirmation.
